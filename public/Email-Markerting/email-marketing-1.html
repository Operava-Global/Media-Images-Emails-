Now that your Spacemail mailbox is up and running, let’s walk you through its main security features so you can be sure your mailbox is safe.

1. Mark as spam

Our in-house anti-spam protection — Jellyfish — works 24/7 in the background, protecting your inbox from spam and malicious emails. At the same time, you can decide for yourself what stays or goes. Just click on Mark as spam (or Not spam if it’s an item already in your spam folder) and Jellyfish will adjust to your choices over time.'

2. Creating blocklist/acceptlist rules

If you need an instant block, you can block senders, choosing from a wide range of parameters, like email address, domain, TLD, and more. To do this, head to the dedicated Jellyfish spam management tool , accessed via your Spacemail Manager .

Go to Spacemail Manager

It’s possible to check logs of all email addresses associated with your domain, too. This can help track what’s being caught in spam nets and what’s being delivered to your inbox. You can block or accept senders, or mailboxes, right away with this option.

3. Encrypted emails

Add an extra level of security to your sensitive message with password-protected emails . To encrypt your message, simply click on the lock icon when composing it and proceed with setting a password.

4. Aliases

Keep your primary inbox hidden with email aliases. Use a different alias every time you sign up for a service or share your email. Track who’s sending you messages and spot unusual activity fast.
And when you need to send an email? Send from your alias so your primary inbox stays hidden. You decide what to share, and what to keep private.

Create an alias

5. Devices tracking & activity log

You can view logs of account activity, like login attempts , and signed-in devices . These logs allow you to see detailed information about each session and you can also sign out of all devices with a single click, ensuring your account remains secure.
